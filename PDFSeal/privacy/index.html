English

PDFSeal (“the App”) is an offline PDF utility. We do not operate a custom server that receives your PDFs, signatures, or form data.

Data stored on your device

- PDF files you import, annotations, and signature images are stored locally in the App’s sandbox.

- A Pro entitlement flag and free-export counter may be stored in UserDefaults.

- Purchases and receipts are processed by Apple; we do not receive your payment card details.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect personal information for analytics advertising profiles, and we do not sell your data. The App does not require an account.

Optional system features

If you share an exported PDF via the system share sheet, the destination app or service you choose may process that file under their own terms.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change. Continued use means you accept the update.

简体中文

签章匣（“本应用”）是一款离线 PDF 工具。我们不运营用于接收你的 PDF、签名或表单数据的自建服务器。

存储在你设备上的数据

- 你导入的 PDF、批注与签名图片保存在应用沙盒本地。

- Pro 权益标记与免费导出次数可能保存在 UserDefaults。

- 购买与收据由 Apple 处理；我们不会收到你的支付卡信息。

我们不收集的数据

我们不为广告画像收集个人信息，也不出售你的数据。本应用无需账号。

可选系统功能

若你通过系统分享导出 PDF，目标应用/服务按其自身条款处理该文件。

儿童隐私

本应用不以 13 岁以下儿童为目标用户。

变更

我们可能更新本政策；生效日期见文首。继续使用即表示接受更新。
