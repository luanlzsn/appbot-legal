中文

1. 我们是谁

酒窖本（CellarKit）是一款帮助你在设备上管理酒柜/酒窖台账的 iOS 应用：记录酒瓶与适饮期、格位图定位、品鉴笔记与购入流水，并可选调度本地适饮提醒。我们不运营用于存储你酒窖内容的自建业务服务器，也不提供酒类电商、配送或拍卖估值 API，更不提供社交公开酒窖网络。

2. 我们收集哪些信息

就开发者可控的「收集并传到开发者服务器」而言：我们基本上不收集你的个人信息或酒窖明细。

数据说明
你输入的酒窖内容（酒款名称、生产商、年份、格位、价格、品鉴笔记、可选本地照片、条码字符串等）保存在**你的设备本地**（如 SwiftData）。开发者**无法**像运营自建后台那样常规读取这些内容。
相机/相册（可选）仅用于在设备上拍摄或选取酒瓶/酒标照片，以及可选的本地文字识别建议；图像默认留在设备。
本地通知相关适饮期提醒由系统在设备上调度；我们不通过自建推送服务器发送营销推送。
应用内购买终身 Pro 等交易由 **Apple** 处理。我们通过 StoreKit 获知你是否已解锁权益，**不单独收集并保存你的银行卡号**。
诊断与分析**未接入**第三方广告或分析 SDK。
账号**无**开发者自建账号体系；你无需向我们注册用户名密码。
可选 iCloud 同步若你日后在设置中开启并由系统提供私有 iCloud/CloudKit 同步，数据经 **Apple** 的 iCloud 基础设施在你的设备间同步；开发者不运营独立同步服务器。当前版本以本机存储为主。

我们不会为提供核心功能而：出售你的酒窖清单、用你的数据做跨 App 追踪广告、连接银行、或建立公共社交酒窖资料。

3. 我们如何使用信息

在设备本地，数据仅用于：展示与编辑酒窖台账、格位定位、适饮状态与 Tonight 列表、品鉴与购入流水、CSV 导入导出（按你的操作）、以及根据 Apple 提供的购买状态解锁 Pro 功能。

4. 共享与第三方

  • Apple：提供 App Store、StoreKit 支付、系统通知、（若启用）iCloud 等基础设施。
  • 我们不向广告商出售个人信息。
  • 法律要求或为保护权利所必需时，可能在法律允许范围内披露；因无自建业务库存储你的酒窖正文，实际可披露范围通常极为有限。

5. 数据保留与删除

  • 本地数据：你可在应用内删除条目或「删除全部本地数据」；卸载应用通常会删除设备上的应用数据（以系统行为为准）。
  • Pro 权益与 Apple 账户购买记录相关联；恢复购买依赖你的 Apple ID。

6. 儿童与酒精内容

本应用面向一般成年消费者管理个人收藏台账，未专门面向儿童设计。应用本身不售酒；请遵守你所在地区关于酒精相关内容的规定，并由监护人合理管理设备与 Apple ID。

7. 你的选择

  • 可拒绝相机、相册或通知权限，核心台账仍可手动使用。
  • 可不购买 Pro，在免费瓶数与单一空间限额内使用。
  • 可通过系统设置管理通知、面容 ID 与屏幕时间等。

8. 国际用户

App Store 由 Apple 在全球基础设施上运营。我们不另设自建跨国业务数据库存放你的酒窖内容。

9. 政策更新

English

1. Who we are

CellarKit (Chinese name: 酒窖本) is an iOS app that helps you manage a personal drink-cellar ledger on device: bottles and drink windows, rack/fridge slot maps, tasting notes, purchase history, and optional local drink-window reminders. We do not operate a custom business backend that stores your cellar contents, do not run alcohol commerce or delivery, and do not provide a public social cellar network.

2. What we collect

Regarding information the developer collects onto developer-operated servers: we essentially collect little to none.

DataDetails
Cellar content you enter (names, producers, vintages, slots, prices, tasting notes, optional local photos, barcode strings)Stored **on your device** (e.g. SwiftData). We do not routinely read that content via a developer backend.
Camera / Photos (optional)Used on-device to capture or pick bottle/label photos and optional on-device text suggestions; images stay on device by default.
Local notificationsScheduled on-device; we do not run our own marketing push server.
In-App PurchasesHandled by **Apple**. We learn whether you unlocked Pro via StoreKit. We do **not** collect your card number ourselves.
Analytics / ads**No** third-party ad or analytics SDK.
Accounts**No** developer-operated user accounts.
Optional iCloud syncIf you later enable private iCloud/CloudKit sync, sync uses **Apple** iCloud infrastructure between your devices. The current build is primarily on-device.

We do not, for core features: sell your cellar list, track you across apps for ads, link banks, or publish public social cellars.

3. How we use information

On-device: to show and edit your cellar ledger, locate bottles, compute drink-window states and Tonight lists, keep tastings and purchase history, import/export CSV at your request, and unlock Pro based on Apple purchase state.

4. Sharing and third parties

  • Apple: App Store, StoreKit, system notifications, and (if enabled) iCloud.
  • We do not sell personal information to advertisers.
  • We may disclose information when legally required; without a developer backend holding your cellar text, practical disclosure is typically very limited.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Local data: delete items in-app or use delete-all; uninstalling usually removes app data (per system behavior).
  • Pro entitlement follows your Apple ID purchases; restore uses your Apple ID.

6. Children and alcohol-related content

The app is for general adult collectors’ personal ledgers and is not directed at children. The app does not sell alcohol. Guardians should manage devices and Apple IDs appropriately.

7. Your choices

  • You may deny camera, photos, or notification permission and still use manual ledger features.
  • You may stay on the free tier (bottle and single-space limits).
  • System Settings control notifications, Face ID, and Screen Time.

8. International users

The App Store runs on Apple’s global infrastructure. We do not operate a separate developer database of your cellar contents.

9. Updates

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date on this page. Material changes may also be noted in-app or on the product page where appropriate.
