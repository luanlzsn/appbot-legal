Privacy Policy — Sky Folio

Sky Folio (星穹本) keeps the chart math, the star catalog, and the observation ledger on this device. There is no account and no custom server.

Location and camera

Location, when you allow it, turns the chart for the latitude where you stand. The camera, when you allow it, lays an identifying reticle on the live view. Neither image nor coordinate is uploaded by Sky Folio. If you decline either permission, Chart mode and a city or typed coordinates still work.

Ledger

Ledger photos come from the system photo library and stay in the on-device ledger. You can leave location off a new note, and you can delete the ledger.

Purchases

Sky Folio Pro is a non-consumable lifetime unlock (com.luan.skyfolio.pro.lifetime) processed by Apple. Sky Folio does not receive your payment card number. Restore uses StoreKit on your Apple ID.

Tracking

Sky Folio does not run a third-party analytics or advertising identifier SDK.

Contact

Questions about this policy: luanlzsn@gmail.com.

中文

隐私政策 — 星穹本

星穹本（Sky Folio）把星图计算、星表和观测手账留在这台设备上。没有账号，也没有自建服务器。

定位与相机

你允许定位时，它只用来按纬度转动星图。你允许相机时，它只用来把指认准星叠在实景上。星穹本不上传画面或坐标。拒绝这两项时，仍可用星图模式，并选择城市或输入坐标。

手账

手账照片来自系统相册，保存在本机手账里。新笔记可以不附地点，也可以清空手账。

购买

星穹本 Pro 是一次性的非消耗型内购（com.luan.skyfolio.pro.lifetime），由 Apple 处理。星穹本拿不到你的支付卡号。恢复购买通过 StoreKit 和你的 Apple ID 完成。

追踪

星穹本没有第三方统计或广告标识 SDK。

联系

关于本政策的问题：luanlzsn@gmail.com。
