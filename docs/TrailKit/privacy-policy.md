## 中文

### 1. 我们是谁

山径本（TrailKit）是一款帮助你在**设备上**规划路线、记录户外活动、整理故事章节与本机地图包的 iOS（及配套 Apple Watch）应用。我们**不运营用于存储你轨迹或故事的自建业务服务器**，也**不提供社交关注流、公开排行榜或第三方轨迹社区上传**。

### 2. 我们收集哪些信息

就开发者可控的「收集并传到开发者服务器」而言：**我们基本上不收集你的个人信息或轨迹内容。**

| 数据 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 位置（When In Use / Always） | 用于路线规划、地图展示与户外活动 GPS 记录。锁屏或口袋中续记时可能请求 **Always**。位置轨迹默认保存在**你的设备本地**；开发者不运营可拉取轨迹的自建后台。 |
| 路线、航点、地图包清单与缓存 | 保存在设备本地（如 SwiftData / 本地文件）。地图包为**本机离线包管理**，不经由开发者自建瓦片服务器。 |
| HealthKit 运动相关数据 | 在你授权后，可读取心率等并写入户外 Workout；数据经 Apple Health 体系，开发者不另建健康云库。 |
| Apple Watch 配套 | 通过 **WatchConnectivity** 等系统能力在 iPhone 与 Watch 间传递会话摘要；不经过开发者服务器。 |
| 故事照片钉选（可选） | 你从相册选择的照片钉在本地故事章节；默认不上传到自建服务器。 |
| 应用内购买 | 终身 Pro 等交易由 **Apple StoreKit** 处理。我们通过 StoreKit 获知是否已解锁权益，**不单独收集银行卡号**。 |
| 诊断与分析 | **未接入**第三方广告或分析 SDK。 |
| 账号 | **无**开发者自建账号体系；无社交登录。 |

我们**不会**为提供核心功能而：出售你的轨迹清单、用你的数据做跨 App 追踪广告、或建立公共户外社交资料。

### 3. 我们如何使用信息

在设备本地，数据仅用于：路线编辑与导入导出、实时/回顾地图、活动记录与故事、地图包管理、Watch 配套会话，以及根据 Apple 提供的购买状态解锁 Pro。

### 4. 共享与第三方

- **Apple**：App Store、StoreKit、MapKit、Core Location、HealthKit、WatchConnectivity 等基础设施。  
- **我们不向广告商出售个人信息。**  
- 你主动导出 GPX/故事卡片并通过系统分享发送时，接收方由你选择。  
- 法律要求或为保护权利所必需时，可能在法律允许范围内披露；因无自建业务库存储你的轨迹正文，实际可披露范围通常极为有限。

### 5. 数据保留与删除

- 本地数据：你可在应用内删除路线、活动、地图包等；卸载通常会删除设备上的应用数据（以系统行为为准）。

- Pro 权益与 Apple 账户购买记录相关联；恢复购买依赖你的 Apple ID。

### 6. 儿童

本应用未专门面向儿童设计。请由监护人合理管理设备与 Apple ID。

### 7. 你的选择

## English

### 1. Who we are

TrailKit is an on-device trail planning, outdoor activity, story, and local map-pack app for iPhone (with an Apple Watch companion). We **do not operate a custom backend** that stores your tracks or stories, and we **do not provide a social feed, public leaderboard, or third-party trail community upload**.

### 2. What we collect

For data the developer controls and sends to a developer server: **we essentially do not collect your personal information or track content.**

| Data | Notes |
|------|------|
| Location (When In Use / Always) | Used for route planning, maps, and outdoor GPS recording. **Always** may be requested so recording can continue when the screen locks. Tracks stay **on your device** by default; we do not run a backend that can pull them. |
| Routes, waypoints, map-pack manifests and caches | Stored locally (e.g. SwiftData / files). Map packs are **on-device offline pack management**, not a developer-operated tile CDN. |
| HealthKit workout-related data | With your permission, may read heart rate and write outdoor workouts via Apple Health. We do not run a separate health cloud. |
| Apple Watch companion | Session summaries move between iPhone and Watch via **WatchConnectivity** and related system APIs — not through a developer server. |
| Story photo pins (optional) | Photos you pick from your library pin onto local story chapters; not uploaded to a custom server by default. |
| In-app purchases | Lifetime Pro and similar are processed by **Apple StoreKit**. We learn unlock status only; we do **not** collect card numbers. |
| Analytics / ads | **No** third-party advertising or analytics SDK. |
| Accounts | **No** developer-operated accounts; no social login. |

We do **not** sell your track inventory, use it for cross-app tracking ads, or run a public outdoor social graph for core features.

### 3. How we use information

On device only: route edit/import/export, live/review maps, activity recording and stories, map-pack management, Watch companion sessions, and Pro unlock based on Apple purchase status.

### 4. Sharing and third parties

- **Apple**: App Store, StoreKit, MapKit, Core Location, HealthKit, WatchConnectivity, and related infrastructure.  
- **We do not sell personal information to advertisers.**  
- When you export GPX/story cards via the system share sheet, recipients are chosen by you.  
- We may disclose information when legally required or to protect rights, within applicable law; without a custom business database of your tracks, practical disclosure scope is usually very limited.

### 5. Retention and deletion

- Local data: delete routes, activities, and packs in-app; uninstalling typically removes app data (per system behavior).  
- Revoke location and Health permissions anytime in system Settings.  
- Pro entitlements follow your Apple ID; use Restore Purchases as needed.

### 6. Children

Not directed at children. Guardians should manage devices and Apple IDs appropriately.

### 7. Your choices

- Decline location, HealthKit, or Photos; related features will be unavailable while other local tools may still work.  
- Stay on free route/activity/pack limits without buying Pro.  
- Manage precise location, background location, and Health in system Settings.

### 8. International users

The App Store runs on Apple’s global infrastructure. We do not operate a separate cross-border business database of your tracks or stories.

### 9. Changes

We may update this policy and the effective date on this page. For material changes we will provide reasonable notice in-app or via the product page where applicable.
