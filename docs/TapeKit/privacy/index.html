中文

1. 我们是谁

录音匣（TapeKit）是一款帮助你在设备上快速录音、整理磁带卡、书签、波形修剪与端上转写的 iOS 应用。我们不运营用于存储你录音内容的自建业务服务器，也不提供云会议机器人或社交音频网络。

2. 我们收集哪些信息

就开发者可控的「收集并传到开发者服务器」而言：我们基本上不收集你的个人信息或录音内容。

数据说明
你创建的录音与元数据（标题、场景、文件夹、书签、转写文本等）保存在你的设备本地（如 SwiftData / Documents）。开发者无法像运营自建后台那样常规读取这些内容。
麦克风仅用于在设备上录制音频；录音文件默认留在设备。
语音识别（可选）使用 Apple Speech 在设备上生成可编辑转写；我们不把音频上传到自建服务器。
面容 ID / 设备密码（可选）仅用于在设备上锁定应用入口；生物特征数据由系统保管，开发者不采集。
应用内购买终身 Pro 等交易由 Apple 处理。我们通过 StoreKit 获知是否已解锁权益，不单独收集银行卡号。
诊断与分析未接入第三方广告或分析 SDK。
账号无开发者自建账号体系。
可选 iCloud 同步若你在设置中开启，数据经 Apple iCloud/CloudKit 在你的设备间同步；开发者不运营独立同步服务器。当前版本以本机存储为主。

我们不会为提供核心功能而：出售你的录音清单、用你的数据做跨 App 追踪广告、或建立公共社交录音资料。

3. 我们如何使用信息

在设备本地，数据仅用于：录制与回放、整理文件夹与书签、修剪与转写、搜索、按你的操作分享/导出，以及根据 Apple 提供的购买状态解锁 Pro。

4. 共享与第三方

  • Apple：App Store、StoreKit、Speech、（若启用）iCloud 等基础设施。
  • 我们不向广告商出售个人信息。
  • 法律要求或为保护权利所必需时，可能在法律允许范围内披露；因无自建业务库存储你的录音正文，实际可披露范围通常极为有限。

5. 数据保留与删除

  • 本地数据：你可在应用内删除单条或「删除全部录音」；卸载应用通常会删除设备上的应用数据（以系统行为为准）。
  • Pro 权益与 Apple 账户购买记录相关联；恢复购买依赖你的 Apple ID。

6. 儿童

本应用未专门面向儿童设计。请由监护人合理管理设备与 Apple ID。

7. 你的选择

  • 可拒绝麦克风、语音识别或面容锁权限；未授权时相关功能不可用，其余本地整理仍可使用。
  • 可不购买 Pro，在免费磁带/文件夹/书签限额内使用。
  • 可通过系统设置管理权限与屏幕时间等。

8. 国际用户

App Store 由 Apple 在全球基础设施上运营。我们不另设自建跨国业务数据库存放你的录音内容。

9. 政策更新

我们可能更新本政策。更新后会在本页调整生效日；若变更对你有重大影响，我们将在合理范围内通过应用内说明或 App Store 产品页等方式提示（如适用）。

English

1. Who we are

TapeKit (Chinese name: 录音匣) is an iOS app that helps you capture and organize voice memos on device: scene-labeled tapes, folders, bookmarks, waveform trim, and optional on-device transcripts. We do not operate a custom business backend that stores your recordings, and do not provide a cloud meeting bot or public social audio network.

2. What we collect

Regarding information the developer collects onto developer-operated servers: we essentially collect little to none.

DataDetails
Recordings and metadata you createStored on your device (e.g. SwiftData / Documents). We do not routinely read that content via a developer backend.
MicrophoneUsed on-device to record audio; files stay on device by default.
Speech recognition (optional)Uses Apple Speech on device for editable transcripts; we do not upload audio to a developer server.
Face ID / device passcode (optional)Used only to gate the app on device; biometrics stay with the system.
In-App PurchasesHandled by Apple. We learn whether you unlocked Pro via StoreKit. We do not collect your card number ourselves.
Analytics / adsNo third-party ad or analytics SDK.
AccountsNo developer-operated user accounts.
Optional iCloud syncIf enabled, sync uses Apple iCloud infrastructure between your devices. The current build is primarily on-device.

We do not, for core features: sell your recording list, track you across apps for ads, or publish public social audio profiles.

3. How we use information

On-device: to record and play, organize folders and bookmarks, trim and transcribe, search, share/export at your request, and unlock Pro based on Apple purchase state.

4. Sharing and third parties

  • Apple: App Store, StoreKit, Speech, and (if enabled) iCloud.
  • We do not sell personal information to advertisers.
  • We may disclose information when legally required; without a developer backend holding your recordings, practical disclosure is typically very limited.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Local data: delete individual tapes or “Delete all tapes” in Settings; uninstalling usually removes app data (per system behavior).
  • Pro entitlements follow your Apple ID purchase history; restore uses your Apple ID.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children. Guardians should manage devices and Apple IDs appropriately.

7. Your choices

  • You may deny microphone, speech, or Face ID permissions; related features stay unavailable while other local tools remain usable.
  • You may stay on the free tier within tape/folder/bookmark caps.
  • System Settings control permissions and Screen Time.

8. International users

The App Store runs on Apple’s global infrastructure. We do not operate a separate developer database of your recordings.

9. Updates

We may update this policy and change the effective date on this page. Material changes may also be noted in-app or on the product page where appropriate.
