中文

1. 我们是谁

对照盏（Pairing Cups）是一款帮助你在设备上并排对照冲煮的 iOS 应用：把最好的一杯钉在左边，右边记下下一次的粉量、研磨、水温、时间与风味。我们不运营用于存储你冲煮记录的自建业务服务器，也不提供账号、云同步、社交社区或云端配方目录。

2. 我们收集哪些信息

就开发者可控的「收集并传到开发者服务器」而言：我们基本上不收集你的个人信息或冲煮明细。

数据说明
你输入的内容（冲煮笺、批次名、参数、风味、备注等）保存在你的设备本地（SwiftData / 本地文件）。开发者无法像运营自建后台那样常规读取这些内容。
导出与导入仅在你主动导出时生成文件；只有你再通过系统分享或文件 App 发出，文件才会离开设备。导入在本地完成。
应用内购买终身内购「并排盏托」等交易由 Apple 处理。我们通过 StoreKit 获知你是否已解锁权益，不单独收集并保存你的银行卡号。
可选本地通知若你在设置中开启，提醒由系统在本机发送；默认关闭。不是社交推送。
诊断与分析未接入第三方广告或分析 SDK。
账号无开发者自建账号体系；你无需向我们注册用户名密码。
云同步与健康数据本版本不使用 CloudKit / iCloud 同步，也不使用 HealthKit。记录留在本机。

我们不会为提供核心功能而：出售你的冲煮记录、用你的数据做跨 App 追踪广告、或建立公共社交配方资料。

3. 我们如何使用信息

在设备本地，数据仅用于：展示双杯对照、保存你的冲煮笺与偏好，以及根据 Apple 提供的购买状态解锁「并排盏托」功能。

4. 共享与第三方

  • Apple：提供 App Store、StoreKit 支付等基础设施。
  • 我们不向广告商出售个人信息。
  • 法律要求或为保护权利所必需时，可能在法律允许范围内披露；因无自建业务库存储你的冲煮正文，实际可披露范围通常极为有限。

5. 数据保留与删除

  • 本地数据：你可在应用内删除笺或清空数据，或通过系统卸载删除应用数据（以系统行为为准）。
  • 「并排盏托」权益与 Apple 账户购买记录相关联；恢复购买依赖你的 Apple ID，请使用应用内「恢复购买」。

6. 儿童

应用面向一般冲煮用户，不专门面向儿童收集数据。

7. 国际与变更

我们可能更新本政策并在本页修改生效日。重大变更时尽量在应用内或本页提示。继续使用视为知悉更新（法律另有规定除外）。

English

1. Who we are

Pairing Cups (对照盏) is an iOS app that helps you compare brews on your device: pin your best cup on the left, and record the next dose, grind, temperature, time, and taste on the right. We do not operate a custom business server that stores your brew notes, and we do not provide accounts, cloud sync, a social community, or a cloud recipe catalog.

2. What we collect

Regarding developer-controlled “collection sent to developer servers”: we essentially do not collect your personal information or brew details.

DataNotes
Content you enter (slips, batch names, parameters, taste, notes)Stored locally on your device (SwiftData / local files). The developer cannot routinely read this as if running a custom backend.
Export and importFiles are created only when you export them. They leave the device only if you share them through the system share sheet or Files. Import stays on device.
In-App PurchasesThe lifetime unlock “Pairing Saucer” is processed by Apple. We learn entitlement state via StoreKit and do not separately store your card numbers.
Optional local notificationsIf you turn them on in Settings, the system delivers them on this device. They are off by default and are not social push notifications.
Analytics / adsNo third-party ad or analytics SDK.
AccountsNo developer-operated account system.
Cloud sync and HealthKitThis version does not use CloudKit / iCloud sync and does not use HealthKit. Notes stay on this device.

3. How we use information

On device only: show the two-cup comparison, save your slips and preferences, and unlock Pairing Saucer based on Apple purchase state.

4. Sharing

  • Apple provides the App Store and StoreKit.
  • We do not sell personal information to advertisers.
  • Disclosure only as required by law or to protect rights, within legal limits. Because we do not store your brew notes on a developer server, what can actually be disclosed is usually very limited.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Local data: delete slips or clear data in-app, or uninstall the app (subject to system behavior).
  • Pairing Saucer entitlement is tied to your Apple ID purchases; use in-app “Restore Purchases” to restore.

6. Children

The app is for general brewing users and is not directed at children for data collection.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and the effective date on this page. Continued use constitutes awareness of updates where permitted by law.
