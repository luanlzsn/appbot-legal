中文

1. 我们是谁

安心发图（SafeShare Photo）是一款帮助你在设备上检测并遮挡照片中人脸、文字、车牌等隐私区域，并在导出前核对清单、移除元数据后再分享的 iOS 应用。我们不运营用于存储你照片的自建业务服务器，也不提供云端打码或社交公开图库。

2. 我们收集哪些信息

就开发者可控的「收集并传到开发者服务器」而言：我们基本上不收集你的个人信息或测光明细。

数据说明

照片图库 / 分享扩展（可选）用于选择待处理照片；检测与遮挡留在设备，不上传到开发者服务器。可拒绝相册写入权限（仍可分享处理后的图片）。
应用内购买终身 Pro 等交易由 Apple 处理。我们通过 StoreKit 获知你是否已解锁权益，不单独收集并保存你的银行卡号。
诊断与分析未接入第三方广告或分析 SDK。

可选 iCloud 同步若你日后在设置中开启并由系统提供私有 iCloud/CloudKit 同步，数据经 Apple 的 iCloud 基础设施在你的设备间同步；开发者不运营独立同步服务器。当前版本以本机存储为主。

我们不会为提供核心功能而：出售你的照片、用你的数据做跨 App 追踪广告、或建立公共图库。

3. 我们如何使用信息

在设备本地，数据仅用于：本地检测与打码、导出前隐私核对、保存草稿/模板与偏好，以及根据 Apple 购买状态解锁 Pro 功能。

  • Apple：提供 App Store、StoreKit 支付、Photos、Share Extension 与（若启用）iCloud 等基础设施。

  • 法律要求或为保护权利所必需时，可能在法律允许范围内披露；因无自建业务库存储你的测光正文，实际可披露范围通常极为有限。

5. 数据保留与删除

  • 本地数据：你可在应用内删除条目或通过系统卸载删除应用数据（以系统行为为准）。

6. 儿童

English

1. Who we are

SafeShare Photo (安心发图) is an iOS app that helps you detect and redact faces, text, license plates, and other privacy regions on device, then review an export checklist and remove metadata before sharing. We do not operate a custom business server that stores your photos, and we do not provide cloud redaction or a public social gallery.

2. What we collect

Regarding developer-controlled “collection sent to developer servers”: we essentially do not collect your personal information or metering details.

DataNotes
Photos you choose, redaction regions, drafts/templates, and preferencesStored locally on your device. The developer cannot routinely read this as if running a custom backend.
Photo library / Share Extension (optional)Used to pick photos for local redaction; processing stays on device and is not uploaded to developer servers. You may deny Photos add permission and still share the processed image.
In-App PurchasesProcessed by Apple. We learn entitlement state via StoreKit and do not separately store your card numbers.
Analytics / adsNo third-party ad or analytics SDK.
AccountsNo developer-operated account system.
Optional iCloud syncIf you later enable private iCloud/CloudKit sync, data moves through Apple infrastructure. Default is local-only.

3. How we use information

On device only: run on-device detection and redaction, export-checklist review, save drafts/templates and preferences, and unlock Pro based on Apple purchase state.

4. Sharing

  • Apple provides App Store, StoreKit, Photos, Share Extension, and optional iCloud.
  • We do not sell personal information to advertisers.
  • Disclosure only as required by law or to protect rights, within legal limits.

5. Retention & deletion

  • Local data: delete entries in-app or uninstall.
  • Pro entitlement is tied to your Apple ID purchases; use Restore in-app.

6. Children

The app is for people who want to protect photo privacy before sharing and is not directed at children for data collection.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and the effective date on this page. Continued use constitutes awareness of updates where permitted by law.
