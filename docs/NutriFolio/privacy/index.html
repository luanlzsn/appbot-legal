Privacy Policy — Nutri Folio

Nutri Folio (营养查本) is an on-device food-composition reference. There is no Nutri Folio account and no custom server. Your queries, notes, and labels are not uploaded to a developer backend.

What stays on the device

The food pack ships inside the app and is read locally. Labels, favorites, today notes, compare sets, and preferences stay on this iPhone (SwiftData and system preferences). Uninstalling the app removes that local data. There is no cloud sync.

Purchases

Nutri Folio Pro is a non-consumable lifetime unlock (com.luan.nutrifolio.pro.lifetime) processed by Apple. Receipts and payment details stay with Apple. Restore uses StoreKit on your Apple ID.

Health

Writing to Apple Health is optional, off by default, and Pro-only. While the switch is off, the app does not create a Health store and does not request authorization. When you turn it on, the app only writes dietary energy, protein, carbohydrates, and fat for the days you logged. It does not read other Health records. Nutri Folio is not a medical device.

Network

Looking up foods, writing notes, and comparing items do not need a network. Opening a legal page or the USDA attribution page uses the system browser for a public URL. The app has no third-party analytics or advertising identifier SDK.

Children

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: luanlzsn@gmail.com.

中文

隐私政策 — 营养查本

营养查本（Nutri Folio）是装在你手机里的离线成分表。没有账号，也没有自建服务器。查询、便签和标签不会上传到开发者后台。

留在设备上的内容

食材库随应用打包，只在本地读取。你写的标签、收藏、今日便签、对比组和偏好存在本机（SwiftData 与系统偏好）。卸载应用会去掉这些内容。没有云同步。

购买

终身解锁 com.luan.nutrifolio.pro.lifetime 由 Apple 处理。收据和付款信息留在 Apple。恢复购买通过 StoreKit 和你的 Apple ID 完成。

健康

写入 Apple 健康是可选项，默认关闭，且只对 Pro 开放。开关关闭时，应用不会创建健康存储，也不会请求授权。打开之后，应用只写入你记录的那几天的膳食能量、蛋白质、碳水化合物和脂肪，不读取其他健康记录。营养查本不是医疗设备。

网络

查食材、记便签、看对比都不需要网络。打开法律页或 USDA 署名页时，系统浏览器会访问对应的公开网址。应用不含第三方统计或广告标识 SDK。

儿童

本应用不是面向儿童的服务，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

联系

关于本政策的问题：luanlzsn@gmail.com。
