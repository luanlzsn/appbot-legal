English

1. Who we are

Go Marks (Chinese name: 换棒记) is an iOS app that helps you view a relay exchange-zone card, place a white-tape start mark, and write this-mark’s verdict on that card, on device. We do not operate a custom business backend that stores your zone cards or verdicts, and we do not provide a cloud training community or cross-user network.

2. What we collect

Regarding information the developer collects onto developer-operated servers: we do not collect your personal information or zone-card records.

DataNotes
Zone cards, mark positions, and this-mark verdicts you createStored locally on your device. The developer cannot routinely read this as if running a custom backend. There is no account.
Unit and language preferencesStored locally on your device.
In-App PurchasesThe optional Mark Sleeve (标记夹) is processed by Apple. We learn entitlement state via StoreKit and do not separately collect card numbers.
Diagnostics and analyticsNo third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. No cookies, no profiling.
AccountsNo developer-operated account system.

We do not, as part of core features, sell your zone-card records, use your data for cross-app tracking ads, or build a public training profile.

3. How we use information

On device, data is used only to show the exchange-zone card, move the white-tape mark, record this-mark’s verdict, and unlock Mark Sleeve based on Apple’s purchase state.

4. Sharing and third parties

  • Apple: App Store, StoreKit, and related infrastructure.
  • We do not sell personal information to advertisers.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, to the extent legally permitted; because we do not store your zone-card content on a developer server, what we could disclose is typically very limited.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Local data: you can delete zone cards in the app; uninstalling usually removes the app’s data on that device (subject to system behavior).
  • Mark Sleeve entitlements are tied to your Apple account purchases; restore depends on your Apple ID.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information. Guardians should manage the device and Apple ID.

7. Your choices

  • You may use the app without buying Mark Sleeve, within the free features.
  • You can manage permissions and Screen Time in system settings.

8. International users

The App Store is operated by Apple on global infrastructure. We do not operate a separate cross-border business database of your zone-card content.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date on this page. If a change is material, we will reasonably notify you in the app or on the App Store product page where applicable.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, email luanlzsn@gmail.com.
