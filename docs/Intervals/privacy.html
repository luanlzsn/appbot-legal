中文

1. 我们是谁

起跑块（Starting Block，工程目录名 Intervals）是一款帮助你在设备上编排赛次、上场计时、查看成绩条并管理赛次表的 iOS 应用，可配合 Apple Watch 与主屏小组件使用。我们不运营用于存储你计时或赛次内容的自建业务服务器，也不提供云端训练社区或跨用户分享网络。

2. 我们收集哪些信息

就开发者可控的「收集并传到开发者服务器」而言：我们不收集你的个人信息或计时记录。

数据说明
你创建的赛次、分段与成绩记录保存在你的设备本地。开发者无法像运营自建后台那样常规读取这些内容。没有账号。
Apple Watch（可选）赛次在 iPhone 与 Watch 之间的同步通过 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity 在你的设备间直接完成，不经过我们的服务器。
通知与灵动岛（可选）用于上场计时提醒与进行中的实时活动。由系统在设备上展示；开发者不通过自建服务器推送内容。
主屏小组件在设备上展示赛次或计时摘要；数据留在设备。
语音报数在设备本地合成播放。音频不会离开这台设备，应用也不使用麦克风录音。
后台音频上场进行中可在后台继续播放提示音，仅在设备本地处理。
应用内购买可选的「检录夹板 / Heat Clipboard」由 Apple 处理。我们通过 StoreKit 获知是否已解锁权益，不单独收集银行卡号。
诊断与分析未接入第三方广告或分析 SDK。不使用 Cookie，不做用户画像。
账号无开发者自建账号体系。

我们不会为提供核心功能而：出售你的赛次记录、用你的数据做跨 App 追踪广告、或建立公共训练资料。

3. 我们如何使用信息

在设备本地，数据仅用于：编排与开始赛次、上场计时与报数、成绩条、赛次表编辑，以及根据 Apple 提供的购买状态解锁检录夹板。

4. 共享与第三方

  • Apple：App Store、StoreKit、WatchConnectivity、系统通知与实时活动、小组件等基础设施。
  • 我们不向广告商出售个人信息。
  • 法律要求或为保护权利所必需时，可能在法律允许范围内披露；因无自建业务库存储你的计时正文，实际可披露范围通常极为有限。

5. 数据保留与删除

  • 本地数据：你可在应用内删除记录；卸载应用通常会删除设备上的应用数据（以系统行为为准）。
  • 检录夹板权益与 Apple 账户购买记录相关联；恢复购买依赖你的 Apple ID。

6. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集其信息。请由监护人合理管理设备与 Apple ID。

7. 你的选择

  • 可拒绝通知权限；未授权时上场计时与赛次表仍可在应用内使用。
  • 可不购买检录夹板，在免费范围内使用。
  • 可通过系统设置管理权限、通知与屏幕时间等。

8. 国际用户

App Store 由 Apple 在全球基础设施上运营。我们不另设自建跨国业务数据库存放你的计时内容。

9. 政策更新

English

1. Who we are

Starting Block (Chinese name: 起跑块; engineering directory: Intervals) is an iOS app that helps you build heats, time them on the block, and review a record strip on device, with optional Apple Watch and Home Screen widgets. We do not operate a custom business backend that stores your timing or heat data, and we do not provide a cloud training community or cross-user network.

2. What we collect

Regarding information the developer collects onto developer-operated servers: we do not collect your personal information or timing records.

DataNotes
Heats, splits, and records you createStored locally on your device. The developer cannot routinely read this as if running a custom backend. There is no account.
Apple Watch (optional)Heat sync between iPhone and Watch happens directly between your devices via Apple's WatchConnectivity — never through our servers.
Notifications and Live Activities (optional)Used for on-block timing alerts and an in-progress Live Activity. The system shows these on your device; we do not push content from a developer server.
Home Screen widgetsShow a heat or timing summary on device. Data stays on the device.
Voice countingSynthesized and played on device. Audio never leaves this device, and the app does not record from the microphone.
Background audioCue sounds may continue while a heat is running in the background. Playback stays on device.
In-App PurchasesThe optional Heat Clipboard (检录夹板) is processed by Apple. We learn entitlement state via StoreKit and do not separately collect card numbers.
Diagnostics and analyticsNo third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. No cookies, no profiling.
AccountsNo developer-operated account system.

We do not, as part of core features, sell your heat records, use your data for cross-app tracking ads, or build a public training profile.

3. How we use information

On device, data is used only to build and run heats, count and cue on the block, show the record strip, edit the heat list, and unlock Heat Clipboard based on Apple’s purchase state.

4. Sharing and third parties

  • Apple: App Store, StoreKit, WatchConnectivity, system notifications and Live Activities, widgets, and related infrastructure.
  • We do not sell personal information to advertisers.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, to the extent legally permitted; because we do not store your timing content on a developer server, what we could disclose is typically very limited.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Local data: you can delete records in the app; uninstalling usually removes the app’s data on that device (subject to system behavior).
  • Heat Clipboard entitlements are tied to your Apple account purchases; restore depends on your Apple ID.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information. Guardians should manage the device and Apple ID.

7. Your choices

  • You may deny notification permission; timing and the heat list still work inside the app.
  • You may use the app without buying Heat Clipboard, within the free features.
  • You can manage permissions, notifications, and Screen Time in system settings.

8. International users

The App Store is operated by Apple on global infrastructure. We do not operate a separate cross-border business database of your timing content.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date on this page. If a change is material, we will reasonably notify you in the app or on the App Store product page where applicable.
