Terms of Use — Mat Folio

Mat Folio is a general fitness and mobility practice tool. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a treatment. Stop if you feel pain. Skip any pose that does not suit you. Talk with a qualified professional before you start, especially if you are injured, ill, or pregnant.

License

A free download includes the starter sessions, a core pose atlas, Cork Classic, and a short journal. Mat Folio Pro is a lifetime non-consumable unlock sold by Apple under the product id com.luan.matfolio.pro.lifetime. The price is shown by the App Store before you buy. Restore is available in the app.

Your notes

Journal text is yours and stays on the device in this version. You can delete an entry or clear the journal. Export creates a file you choose to share.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the app to claim a clinical outcome. Do not copy the pose plates or session text into another product as if they were yours.

Changes

These terms can be updated in the app bundle and on the public page at https://lzsn-luan.github.io/mat-folio/terms. The copy stored in the app remains readable offline.
