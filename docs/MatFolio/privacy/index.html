Privacy Policy — Mat Folio

Mat Folio (垫上本) keeps your practice on this iPhone.

What stays on the device

Sessions, pose plates, journal entries, favorites, custom sessions, and settings are stored on the device with SwiftData and system preferences. This version has no account and no Mat Folio server.

Purchases

Lifetime Pro is a non-consumable in-app purchase (com.luan.matfolio.pro.lifetime) processed by Apple. Mat Folio does not receive your payment card number. Restore uses StoreKit on your Apple ID.

Health

Workout writing is off until you turn it on. If you enable it, the app asks Apple for permission to save a yoga workout you finished. It does not read your other Health data. Mat Folio is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or promise an outcome.

Notifications

Practice reminders are optional. If you allow them, the system schedules local notifications on this iPhone. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Speech

Pose cues are spoken by the on-device speech synthesizer. Audio does not leave the phone for that feature.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support address published with the App Store listing for Mat Folio.

隐私政策 — 垫上本

垫上本（Mat Folio）把练习留在这台 iPhone 上。

留在设备上的内容

课次、体式板、手账、收藏、自建课次和设置保存在本机（SwiftData 与系统偏好设置）。这一版没有账号，也没有垫上本的服务器。

购买

Pro 是一次性的非消耗型内购（com.luan.matfolio.pro.lifetime），由 Apple 处理。垫上本拿不到你的支付卡号。恢复购买通过 StoreKit 和你的 Apple ID 完成。

健康

把训练写入「健康」默认关闭。只有你打开之后，应用才会向系统申请保存一次已完成的瑜伽训练。它不读取你的其他健康数据。垫上本不是医疗设备，不诊断、不治疗，也不承诺结果。

通知

练习提醒是可选的。允许之后，系统会在这台 iPhone 上安排本地通知。你可以在应用或系统设置里关掉。

语音

体式提示由设备上的语音合成朗读。这项功能不会为了朗读把音频发到别处。

联系

关于本政策的问题，请使用 App Store 上垫上本商品页公布的支持邮箱。
