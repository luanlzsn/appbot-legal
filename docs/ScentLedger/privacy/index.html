Privacy Policy — Scent Ledger

Scent Ledger (香氛本) is a private perfume cabinet. The collection, fill levels, wear journal, and photos stay on your device.

What stays on the device

  • Bottle records, wear entries, custom occasions, and settings are stored locally with SwiftData.
  • Photos you add are image files in the app’s local storage. They are not uploaded.
  • Export files are created only when you tap Export, and they leave the device only if you share them.
  • An optional PIN is stored as a hash on the device. Face ID or Touch ID is handled by the system. The app does not receive your biometric data.
  • The optional daily reminder is a local notification. It is off until you turn it on.

Purchases

Scent Ledger Pro is a non-consumable in-app purchase (com.luan.scentledger.pro.lifetime) processed by Apple. Scent Ledger does not receive your payment card number. Restore uses StoreKit on your Apple ID.

What does not leave the device

There is no Scent Ledger account, no custom server, and no analytics SDK. Querying your cabinet does not require a network.

Your choices

You can export or delete the journal, or erase the cabinet, from Settings → Data. Deleting the app removes the local store and photos.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support address published with the App Store listing for Scent Ledger (luanlzsn@gmail.com).

中文

隐私政策 — 香氛本

香氛本（Scent Ledger）是私人香柜。藏品、余量、手账和照片都留在你的设备上。

留在设备上的内容

  • 瓶子、佩戴记录、自定义场合和设置用 SwiftData 存在本地。
  • 你添加的照片是应用本地文件，不会上传。
  • 导出文件只在你点导出时生成；只有你主动分享，它才会离开设备。
  • 可选 PIN 以哈希存在设备上。面容 ID 或触控 ID 由系统处理，应用拿不到生物识别数据。
  • 可选的每日提醒是本地通知，默认关闭。

购买

香氛本 Pro 是一次性的非消耗型内购（com.luan.scentledger.pro.lifetime），由 Apple 处理。香氛本拿不到你的支付卡号。恢复购买通过 StoreKit 和你的 Apple ID 完成。

不会离开设备的内容

没有香氛本账号，没有自建服务器，也没有分析 SDK。查香柜不需要网络。

你可以做什么

在设置 → 数据里可以导出手账、清空手账或清空香柜。删除应用会去掉本地数据库和照片。

联系

关于本政策的问题，请使用 App Store 上香氛本商品页公布的支持邮箱（luanlzsn@gmail.com）。
